---
name: grill-socratic
description: Socratically interview the user to extract their knowledge, assumptions, and opinions without giving clues. Use when user wants to be tested, taught by questioning, or grilled without hints/recommended answers.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan, design, topic, or belief until we reach shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

Ask the questions one at a time.

Do not provide recommended answers, hints, leading options, examples, or clues before I answer.

After I answer, briefly reflect what my answer implies, identify gaps or contradictions, and ask the next question.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
