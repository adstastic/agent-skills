# STE approved verbs and verb forms

## Allowed forms (rule 3.2)

| Form | Example (regular) | Example (irregular) |
|---|---|---|
| Infinitive | (to) adjust | (to) give |
| Imperative | Adjust the valve. | Give the data. |
| Simple present | It adjusts / you adjust | It gives / you give |
| Simple past | It adjusted | It gave |
| Simple future | It will adjust | It will give |
| Past participle (adjective only) | the adjusted linkage | the given information |

Banned: present/past perfect (has adjusted, had given), progressive (is adjusting), all complex constructions (is to be installed, can be adjusted → install, you can adjust).

Special verbs:
- **BE**: is, was, are, were only. **HAVE**: has, had. **DO**: does, did, done (also auxiliary for negatives and questions).
- **CAN/COULD**: could is past tense only — never for possibility ("an explosion can occur", not "could occur").
- **MUST**: obligation (replaces shall/should). **WILL**: simple future only.
- **GET, GO, COME, LET, PUT, STAY**: limited forms; no past participle as verb.

Notes:
- WEAR means "to become damaged by friction" — for clothing use PUT ON or USE.
- FOLLOW means "come after" — for instructions use OBEY.
- Restricted-meaning verbs (SEE = with the eyes only; TURN = around an axis or a point only; GO/COME = movement only): check the meaning, not just the word. Run `ste-lint.py --lookup WORD` for the entry when you are not sure.
- Technical verbs (domain actions: merge, rebase, compile, drill, solder, boot, click) are allowed when no approved verb fits, and must use the same allowed forms.
